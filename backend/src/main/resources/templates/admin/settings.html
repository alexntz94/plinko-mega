Player features

Hold & Drop: holding the drop button plays round after round until the player lets go (it stops by itself on low balance and big wins). Some markets ban autoplay: switch it off there. The other options are what some markets require for responsible gambling: set them as the market's rules say. Operators can make these stricter; only admins can loosen them.
