Bonus meter

Every paid ball that lands on a big prize charges the player's meter and puts part of its bet into a pot. A full meter pays the pot as a MEGA SPIN of the gold wheel, straight away in the same round: the pot times the segment it stops on. The meter is kept per player between sessions.

Gates

Three gate bars, each three pegs wide, stand on every board. The server decides each ball's path; every bar the path crosses multiplies the ball's prize by the boost, so a ball through all three pays the boost cubed.

Off

Wheel

Two SPIN slots on every board (the pair that a ball lands in closest to 4 times in 100). A ball landing there keeps its prize and also spins the wheel, which pays that ball's win again times the segment it stops on.

MEGA BALL

Some balls drop as a gold MEGA BALL. Wherever it lands, its prize (gates included) is multiplied by the MEGA BALL's own multiplier, drawn when the round is played.

Arsenal (power-ups players buy)

A player can buy one power-up for a round. Each is priced per board and risk so the round still returns the table's RTP (prices are worked out by the server; the round costs bet x balls x the price). Some markets do not allow paid features: switch them off there.
